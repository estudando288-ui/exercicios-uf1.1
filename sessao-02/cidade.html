My City is Praia

It is the capital of Cape Verde, an island country in West Africa

Praia is known for its beautiful beaches, culture, and history.

If visitors came to my city, I would show them the city center, Quebra Canela Beach, and Cidade Velha. I would also invite them to try our traditional food and enjoy Cape Verdean music.
